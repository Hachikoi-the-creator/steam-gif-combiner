Steam Animated Showcase

Turn your GIFs into a Steam profile showcase

Drop your GIFs below and get a single wide, animated GIF with all of them playing side by side — sized and auto-compressed under 5 MB to fit a Steam Artwork Showcase on your profile.

How to name your files

  • Every file must be a .gif.
  • Name them with numbers only, in order: 1.gif, 2.gif, 3.gif, …, n.gif.
  • Numbers must start at 1 and be consecutive — no gaps, no duplicates.
  • The order you number them is the left-to-right order in the result.

    Done!

    Download combined.gif

    How to add it to your Steam profile

    Once you've downloaded your combined.gif, follow these steps to set it as a Featured Artwork Showcase.

    1. 1

      Go to your Steam profile in any web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Brave), then click on Artwork.

    2. 2

      Select Upload Artwork.

    3. 3

      Give it a name (it will appear on your profile), select the downloaded GIF, and confirm that you own the GIF.

    4. 4

      Go back to your profile and select Edit Profile.

    5. 5

      Open Showcase Manager. With the arrows on the left, pick a section you don't mind replacing, and in the highlighted dropdown select Featured Artwork Showcase. Now just select the GIF you uploaded, save, and enjoy!

    All processing runs locally on your machine.
